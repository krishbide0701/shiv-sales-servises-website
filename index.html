Essential RO Purifier Spare Parts

RO Membrane

The core filtration element that removes dissolved solids, heavy metals, pesticides, and micro-contaminants.

Sediment Filter

Traps visible particles like dirt, sand, rust, and mud to protect downstream filters and the RO membrane.

Pre-Carbon Filter

Absorbs chlorine, organic chemicals, and foul odors to enhance taste and extend membrane lifespan.

UV Lamp Assembly

Disinfects water by exposing it to ultraviolet light, neutralizing harmful bacteria and viruses.

RO Booster Pump

Provides the required water pressure for the RO membrane to process raw water efficiently.

TDS Controller

Balances the mineral content in purified water to ensure healthy and natural taste.
